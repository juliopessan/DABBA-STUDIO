01 / The shift

Make the method
repeatable.

The method already worked. Five specialists, each with a clear scope, producing artifacts in the right order.

What was expensive was the driving: a person had to trigger every phase, carry context from one to the next and stitch the output together. DABBA moves that orchestration into the system — without changing the method.

"The value wasn't locked in the agents.
It was locked in the person who knew how to drive them."
